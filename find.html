AI finds themes in your responses

Consult uses artificial intelligence (AI) to review data from open questions and suggest themes.

Your team then review, update and select which themes you will use in the final analysis.

What the AI produces is the starting point for your team to work from. The AI is surfacing suggestions, you will make the decisions in the Finalise themes phase.

We start this step once its confirmed your data is set up correctly.

How AI builds themes

The AI builds themes in 4 stages, it:

  • Reviews all responses and creates initial themes
  • Reviews the list of themes and removes duplicates
  • Reviews the wording to make them easier to understand
  • Sorts themes into parent and child themes (where needed) so that the list has the right granularity of themes

This takes up to 48 hours, depending on the number of responses in your consultation. We will email you when the themes are ready for review.
